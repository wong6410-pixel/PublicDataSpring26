## Data Source

Monthly average temperature data (°F) for Seattle and Miami, 2023.

- NOAA NWS Seattle Climate Data: https://www.weather.gov/wrh/climate?wfo=sew  
- NOAA NWS Miami Monthly Climate Data: https://www.weather.gov/mfl/mia_cliplot  
- NOAA NCEI U.S. Climate Normals (1991–2020): https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/us-climate-normals
