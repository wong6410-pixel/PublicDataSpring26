Two Cities, One Year

Seattle and Miami share a latitude gap of only 17°—but their temperature stories couldn't be more different. Seattle dips to 41 °F in winter and climbs to 74 °F in summer: a 33-degree swing that traces a dramatic arc through the year. Miami barely budges, hovering in a narrow 21-degree band between warmth and heat. This radial chart uses the year as a clock face to make that contrast visceral.
