# HW3 — Interactive Multi Line Chart: Seattle vs. Miami Temperatures

This interactive multi line chart visualizes average monthly temperatures (°F) for 
Seattle, WA and Miami, FL throughout 2023. Seattle stays considerably cooler than 
Miami year-round. Miami maintains warm temperatures even in winter months.

## Interactions

- **Dropdown** — switch between Both, Seattle only, or Miami only. Lines animate in and out.
- **Range Slider** — filter months from January up to any selected month. Chart updates with a smooth transition.

## Data Source

- NOAA NWS Seattle: https://www.weather.gov/wrh/climate?wfo=sew
- NOAA NWS Miami: https://www.weather.gov/mfl/mia_cliplot
- NOAA NCEI U.S. Climate Normals (1991–2020): https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/us-climate-normals
